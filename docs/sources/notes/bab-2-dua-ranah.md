# Bab 2 — Dua ranah: realitas dan preferensi

**Photo files:** p023–p032
**Printed pages seen:** 16, 59–75

## What it establishes

Two domains of information, judged by different standards:

| | Reality domain | Preference domain |
|---|---|---|
| Value of the information | true / false | good / bad, nice / ugly |
| Reference for judgement | check reality (empirical evidence) | a chosen moral/ethical/aesthetic standard |
| How to judge | observe reality | follow the chosen standard |

*(p030 — the book's own summary table)*

Three details that matter more than the table:

1. **Being in the reality domain does not make a claim true.** "Bali is in
   Australia" is a reality-domain claim and it is false. The domain says how to
   check it, not whether it holds. *(p023)*
2. **Preference claims can be near-universal without becoming facts.** The book
   distinguishes preference claims that most people share from claims that are
   empirically checkable. Popularity is not truth. *(p025)*
3. **Most real arguments are the two domains mixed.** The book's worked case is
   a couple arguing about their child, where each side presents a preference as
   if it were a fact. Separating the domains dissolves the argument because the
   two people turn out to want the same thing. *(p031–p032)*

## Maps to our framework

- Stage **Tag**: every claim in a decision is marked fact or preference.
- This is the one stage that needs no arithmetic, no model, and no data — and it
  is the most distinctive thing in the book. Build it early.

## Primary source

Hume, *A Treatise of Human Nature* (1739) — the is–ought gap. In decision
analysis: Keeney separates values from facts explicitly.

## Do not copy

The couple's argument, the "three bags" example, the influencer/jedag-jedug case
(p028), the exercise table (p029).

## Engine relevance

**High, and cheap.** A tag per claim, plus one validation rule: a claim tagged
fact must carry a source or a check; a claim tagged preference must carry whose
preference it is. No computation — which is exactly why it should ship in v1.
